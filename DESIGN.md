## 1. Brand Philosophy & Visual Principles

AWA Group’s brand identity reflects the intersection of **precision food science**, **global supply chain reliability**, and **industrial manufacturing excellence**.

* **Precision & Clarity:** Crisp typography, balanced whitespace, and purposeful contrast communicate scientific rigor and high-standard manufacturing.
* **Vitality & Sustainability:** Vibrant, natural greens balanced against authoritative corporate navies symbolize food safety, agricultural vitality, and sustainable growth.
* **Enterprise Elegance:** Subtle elevation, border delineations, and restrained radius tokens replace noisy gradients with modern corporate sophistication.

## 7. Brand Color Usage Guidelines

### ✅ Do
- **Use `#00A84F` purposefully:** Reserve for primary calls to action, brand validation badges, and key metric emphasizes.
- **Pair Green with Crisp White & Navy:** Maintain a clean, airy corporate atmosphere by surrounding green accents with ample white space and dark navy typography.
- **Maintain High Contrast:** Ensure any text placed over colored backgrounds adheres to minimum WCAG AA contrast standards (minimum 4.5:1 for body copy).
- **Honor Brand Sub-Identities:** Apply the specific B2C palettes (e.g., Sweet & Slim mints, SquEasy ruby fruits) within their dedicated showcases.

### ❌ Don't
- **Never use light gray text on `#00A84F`:** Always pair with solid white (`#FFFFFF`) or deep navy (`#0F172A`).
- **Never oversaturate entire backgrounds with primary green:** Use `#EBF7EE` or `#F7F9FC` for broad background tints instead of raw `#00A84F`.
- **Do not mix conflicting B2C product colors:** Keep SquEasy warm dessert tones distinct from Yalla Drinks beverage palettes to maintain consumer SKU clarity.
